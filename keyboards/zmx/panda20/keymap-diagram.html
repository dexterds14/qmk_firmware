ZMX Panda20 — dexter keymap (VIA)

21-key wireless numpad on a closed vendor QMK fork — never reflashed; the keymap is written to EEPROM over VIA raw HID (via_keymap.py apply, source of truth keymap.txt). Each cell's small corner tag is the key's PHYSICAL stock-layer name, as used in keymap.txt. Dashed ▽ = transparent, blank = no-op / no key. Dotted amber = vendor keycodes: leave in place or wireless switching is lost.
