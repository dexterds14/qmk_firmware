ymdk/ymd09 — dexter keymap

3×3 nav/layer pad, adapted from the ZMX Panda20 layout. The bottom row's layer keys are one-shot with lock (ONESHOT_TAP_TOGGLE 2): tap = next keypress on that layer, double-tap = lock, tap again = unlock, hold = momentary. The whole board glows the active layer's color (which doubles as "one-shot armed" feedback). Dashed ▽ = transparent, blank = no-op.
