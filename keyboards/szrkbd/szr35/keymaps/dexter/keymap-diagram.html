szrkbd/szr35 — dexter keymap

Split 3×5 + 3 thumbs per side (36 keys), ported from the bcorne keymap. Gold-bordered keys are tap-dances (details below the grids). Dashed ▽ = transparent (falls through), blank = no-op. Layer stripe colors match the board's RGB indicator; the board also goes white while Shift is held or one-shot. Generated from keymap.c by gen-diagram.py; edit the keymap and re-run it.
